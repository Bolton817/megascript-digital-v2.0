# MegaScript Digital: Video Assets Directory\n\nThis directory (public/videos/) is designated for client-facing product demos, architectural walkthroughs, and technical media.\n\n## Video Optimization Guidelines\n- Format: MP4 (H.264 video codec, AAC audio) with progressive download, or WebM (VP9).\n- Resolution: 1080p (1920x1080) for desktop showcases; 720p (1280x720) for mobile.\n- Autoplay Videos: Must have muted, playsinline, loop attributes and zero sound on initialization.\n- Size Limit: Keep hosted video files under 10 MB for fast edge delivery via Netlify CDN.\n- Larger Media: For videos exceeding 10 MB, stream via Supabase Storage media bucket or dedicated video CDN.\n
